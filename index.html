I connect requirements, risks, test evidence, and stakeholder decisions instead of treating QA as a final checkpoint.

01

Review requirements, acceptance criteria, designs, dependencies, and unclear business rules.

02

Prioritize

Map risk by customer impact, financial exposure, integration complexity, and change surface.

03

Validate

Combine scenario, exploratory, API, data, cross-platform, and regression testing.

04

Communicate

Report evidence, defect impact, residual risk, and a clear QA recommendation while final release authority remains with the responsible stakeholders.
